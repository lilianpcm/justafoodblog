*Last updated: November 18, 2026*

Just A Food Blog (www.justafoodblog.com) is run by The Shop Curator. We collect as little as possible. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to reach us.

## Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages are useful. When you first visit, a notice asks whether you accept cookies for these statistics.

- **If you accept,** Google Analytics stores cookies in your browser to count visits and pages viewed.
- **If you decline,** no analytics cookies are stored. Google may still receive basic, cookieless signals (such as a page view) that it uses to estimate totals without identifying you.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell your data. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings; the notice will appear again. Learn more in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your email address (free printable)

If you ask for our free printable, we collect the email address you type and the date of your request. We use it to:

- send you the printable download link, and
- send occasional food and diet tips and news about our printables, as you agreed in the form.

Your email is stored on our web host and is never sold or shared for others' marketing. You can stop our emails or ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to {{ site.contact_email }}.

## Links to Etsy

Our product links take you to our shop on Etsy (theshopcurator.etsy.com). Purchases happen on Etsy, under [Etsy's privacy policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/privacy/). We do not see your payment details.

## Hosting and security

The site is hosted by Hostinger. Like any web server, it keeps short technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and to prevent abuse of the form.

## Children

This site is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
