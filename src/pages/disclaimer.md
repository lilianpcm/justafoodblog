General information, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian before changing your diet.

The food lists, guides and printables on Just A Food Blog are **general information for educational purposes**. They are not medical advice and do not replace advice from a doctor, registered dietitian or other qualified professional.

- **Talk to your doctor or dietitian** before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, take medication, have more than one health condition or have been told to follow a specific plan.
- **Diets vary from person to person.** A food that is fine for one person may not be for another. Food allergies and intolerances always come first.
- **Medication and food can interact.** If you take medication, ask your doctor or pharmacist whether any food on a list affects it.
- **We make no promises of results.** Results vary from person to person.

Our printables are **digital PDF files** that you download and print at home. No physical item is shipped.
